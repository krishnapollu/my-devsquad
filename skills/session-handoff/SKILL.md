# Session handoff

Keep `devsquad/state.md` compact and current. Include objective, phase, approved scope, changed files, commands/results, blockers, decisions, and the next three actions. Do not copy large source files or logs into state. On resume, verify the working tree and checkpoint against reality before continuing.
